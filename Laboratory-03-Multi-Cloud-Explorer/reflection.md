# Mission Reflection

## Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

Throughout this mission as a member of the Cloud Evaluation Team, mas naging clear sa akin yung differences ng **AWS**, **Microsoft Azure**, at **Google Cloud Platform**, pati na kung gaano sila ka-similar pagdating sa mga basic services.

Among the three, AWS yung pinaka-na-impress ako. Ang dami nilang services, matagal na sila sa market, at flexible siya for different types of workloads. Yung global infrastructure and free-tier options niya make it easier din for beginners, pero kaya rin niyang mag-handle ng enterprise-level needs.

Kahit magkakaiba sila ng branding and interface, halos same din yung core services ng **AWS**, **Azure**, at **GCP**. Lahat sila may virtual machines, object storage, identity and access management, managed databases, at Kubernetes. Pare-pareho rin silang gumagamit ng pay-as-you-go pricing model at may global network of data centers na naka-organize into regions and availability zones.

Malaking factor din yung business requirements sa pagpili ng right cloud platform. For example, yung startup na limited yung budget and mobile-first yung product, pwedeng mas piliin yung AWS dahil sa free tier and scalability. Yung company naman na gumagamit na ng Microsoft tools like Windows Server and Active Directory, mas bagay yung Azure dahil sa easy integration. Meanwhile, kung AI-focused yung research company, mas okay yung GCP dahil strong siya sa machine learning and data analytics tools like Vertex AI and BigQuery.

As a Cloud Solutions Consultant, natutunan ko na hindi lang about sa pagpili kung alin yung “best” or pinaka-popular na cloud provider. Mas important kung alin yung pinaka-fit sa specific technical and business needs ng client. Kailangan i-balance yung cost, compatibility, scalability, at long-term support bago gumawa ng decision.

Lastly, malaking improvement din yung mission na ’to sa GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio ko. Mas organized na ngayon yung research files, comparison tables, at client recommendation reports ko. Naka-version control din lahat and presented in a more professional way, which feels more aligned sa actual cloud consulting practices.
